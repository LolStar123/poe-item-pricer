the tail matters.

Profit factor is expected gains divided by expected losses. Return/risk is expected profit divided by one-roll standard deviation; it is not annualised Sharpe.

from listings to a decision sheet

  1. describe the item precisely

    Enumerate modifiers, variant pairs and roll ranges so each observation refers to a defined outcome.

  2. collect and record price evidence

    The local residential-proxy collector logs available trade listings under rate controls. Credentials remain local. Price observations retain their collection time and league.

  3. separate a cheap listing from a sensible buy-in

    The buy-in rule uses the median of the ten cheapest listings and reports the floor separately. Missing or suspicious prices remain visible.

  4. keep the workbook linked

    Outcome weights and prices feed expected value, profit, dispersion and downside. Editing an input propagates through the formulas. Coverage gaps stop an incomplete price set being presented as a complete estimate.
